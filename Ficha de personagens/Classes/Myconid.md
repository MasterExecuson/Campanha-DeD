## The Myconid

_A sentient fungus that walks among the folk of Dolmenwood, communing with the silent green and the deeper mycelium below. Born of a spore drift from somewhere stranger than the Wood, the Myconid is a wanderer-philosopher in a body of cap and stipe, slow of speech and patient of mind. Where others see rot, the Myconid sees memory; where others see a mushroom, the Myconid sees a cousin._

### Requirements & Prime Requisite

- **Prime Requisite**: Wisdom
- **Hit Dice**: 1d6 per level (after level 9: +1 hp per level, no Constitution bonus)
- **Maximum Level**: 14
- **Armor**: None (the Myconid's body cannot tolerate restrictive coverings; armor smothers the cap and disrupts spore release)
- **Weapons**: Simple weapons only (club, staff, dagger, sling, hand axe). The Myconid wields these without grace, treating them as tools rather than instruments of skill.

### Natural Armour

The Myconid's tough fungal flesh provides **base AC 13**, improving as their body matures:

- **Levels 1-3**: AC 13
- **Levels 4-7**: AC 14
- **Levels 8-11**: AC 15
- **Levels 12-14**: AC 16

Dexterity modifier applies on top of natural AC.

### Class Abilities

#### Sporecraft

The Myconid summons fungi from the rich loam of their own being. Each day after their rest, they regenerate a number of summonings equal to the value on the table. To summon a mushroom, the Myconid pulls it from their flesh (1 round, requires a free hand). Summoned mushrooms are real, persist after use, and can theoretically be harvested by others, though they wither within 1 turn (10 minutes) if not used immediately.

The Myconid begins play knowing 3 mushrooms (player's choice). They learn 1 new mushroom each level, up to a maximum of 14 known.

#### Spore Puff

A natural ranged attack. The Myconid expels a cluster of hardened spores from a vent on their cap.

- **Damage**: 1d4
- **Range**: 30/60/90 ft
- **Uses Dexterity** for attack and damage rolls
- **At-will**, no daily limit

#### Telepathy (60 ft)

The Myconid projects thoughts directly into the minds of intelligent, sentient creatures within 60 ft. No line of sight required, but same plane only. Communication is **one-way** (the Myconid sends; recipients respond by normal speech, gesture, or their own telepathy).

The transmission carries **concepts, emotions, and concrete imagery, not specific words**. A Myconid cannot transmit a precise password, a poem, or a legal phrase, but can convey _"danger ahead, two armed humans, threat-feeling"_ to any sentient being regardless of language. Numbers up to 10 and simple commands transmit clearly. Abstract or technical content arrives as confused impressions.

The recipient must have Intelligence 3 or greater. _Detect Thoughts_ and similar magic can perceive or block the transmission; mind-shielding effects render the target unreachable.

#### Identify Mushroom (30 ft)

The Myconid can recognise any fungus, mould, or fungal entity within 30 ft on sight, identifying its species, properties, edibility, magical effects, and dangers. This applies to mundane mushrooms, magical fungi, fungal monsters (shriekers, violet fungi, myconids), and fungal spell effects.

#### Fungal Constitution

The Myconid does not require sleep, food, or drink in the conventional sense. Instead, they must **rest for 8 hours in moist soil**, remaining still but conscious throughout (they cannot be ambushed in their sleep, but cannot move or take actions during rest).

- **Resting Soil**: A Myconid can carry up to **3 days' worth of resting soil** in containers (each day's supply takes 1 item slot or 1 stone of encumbrance). Stored soil dries out after 1 week unless refreshed with the equivalent of a waterskin's water.
- **Arid environments** (deserts, dry stone, salt flats) prevent natural rest. Without stored soil, the Myconid cannot regenerate sporecraft uses or recover hit points.
- **Immune to mundane diseases, moulds, and parasites.**
- **Does not bleed** in the conventional sense (relevant to certain magical effects and tracking).

#### Fire Sensitivity

Fungal flesh dries and cracks under flame. The Myconid takes an **extra 1d4 damage** from any fire source (torches, oil flasks, fire-based spells, dragon breath). This is added to whatever damage roll the source produces.

#### Sunlight Sensitivity

Direct sunlight withers the Myconid's senses and reflexes. While in **direct, unfiltered sunlight**, the Myconid suffers **-2 to attack rolls and saving throws**. This penalty does _not_ apply under forest canopy, indoors, in caves, on overcast days, at dawn or dusk, or at night. (In Dolmenwood, where most of the map is shaded by the great wood, this penalty applies only in clearings, on open roads, and during open-sky travel.)
