## The Phyrexian Evangelist

_A herald of the Machine Orthodoxy, the Evangelist walks among the folk of Dolmenwood preaching the gospel of compleation: that flesh is weakness, that the gleaming oil of Phyrexia offers transcendence, and that all who listen may one day be remade. Drawn from a long-buried cult that survived the fall of an evil empire, the Evangelist's body is a partial work, half mortal flesh and half grafted machine. They believe themselves blessed. Most who meet them disagree._

### Requirements & Prime Requisite

- **Prime Requisite**: Wisdom (the Evangelist's faith is their power)
- **Hit Dice**: 1d8 per level (after level 9: +2 hp per level, no Constitution bonus)
- **Maximum Level**: 14
- **Armor**: Any armor compatible with their grafted body (light and medium; heavy armor cannot accommodate exposed grafts)
- **Weapons**: Simple weapons only, plus the Grafted Armament

### Natural Armour

Partially compleated flesh provides **base AC 13**, improving as the body's grafts mature:

- **Levels 1-3**: AC 13
- **Levels 4-7**: AC 14
- **Levels 8-11**: AC 15
- **Levels 12-14**: AC 16

This is the Evangelist's natural AC when unarmored. They may wear light or medium armor, in which case use the armor's AC plus Dex modifier (natural AC does not stack with worn armor; use whichever is higher). Heavy armor (plate) cannot fit over the body's grafts.

### Class Abilities

#### Spellcasting

The Evangelist casts divine spells from the **Cleric spell list**, with Wisdom as their prime requisite. They worship **Phyrexia and the Praetors**, the semi-divine architects of compleation, rather than any conventional deity.

**Reversed spells**: Unlike most clerics, the Evangelist may freely cast either the standard or reversed version of any spell on the list. _Cure Light Wounds_ and _Cause Light Wounds_, _Bless_ and _Curse_, all are accessible. Phyrexian doctrine sees creation and destruction as two faces of the same compleation.

**Phyrexian flavor names**: Spells may be reflavored without mechanical change. _Cure Light Wounds_ becomes _Graft Tissue_. _Bless_ becomes _Anoint with Oil_. _Detect Evil_ becomes _Detect Flesh_ (detecting biological life as the inverse of Phyrexian purity). _Light_ becomes a glowing oil spatter. Players and GMs may rename any spell that fits the theme.

#### Grafted Armament

The Evangelist can permanently graft **one simple weapon** into their body. Common choices: a dagger emerging from the wrist, a club replacing the forearm, a sling integrated into the hand, a mace fused to the knuckles.

- **Cannot be disarmed, sundered, or stolen.**
- **+1 to attack and damage** with the grafted weapon.
- **The grafted limb is permanently occupied** by the weapon. A grafted dagger occupies one hand; a grafted two-handed weapon occupies both. The Evangelist cannot wield a shield in a grafted hand.
- **Replacing or removing** the graft requires a **1-hour ritual** of self-surgery in safe conditions. No risk, no damage, but the Evangelist cannot be in combat or under threat during the ritual.
- **Magical weapons may be grafted**, fusing the enchantment to the Evangelist. A grafted magical weapon retains its magical properties. Removing it via the standard ritual destroys the magical enchantment but leaves the weapon usable as a mundane item.

#### Natural Attack: Oily Strike

When unarmed and not using the grafted weapon, the Evangelist may strike with grafted claws, talons, or hooked appendages. **1d4 damage**, melee, uses Strength for attack and damage. Does not benefit from the Grafted Armament bonus.

#### Mechanical Trance

The Evangelist does not sleep. Instead, they enter an **8-hour mechanical trance**, kneeling or sitting still and praying silently to the Father of Machines. They remain **fully aware** during trance and cannot be ambushed in their sleep. They cannot move or take actions during trance, but they perceive their surroundings normally.

Trance counts as a full rest for purposes of recovering spells and class abilities. Interrupted trance (such as combat starting nearby) requires the Evangelist to begin again, losing whatever portion was completed.

#### Iron Hunger

The Evangelist's body burns metal and oil, not biological food. They must consume:

- **500 g of metal per day**, OR
- **1 vial of oil per day**

Coins count toward the daily requirement (roughly 500 copper or 50 silver = 500 g or 5 gp = 500 g, for simplicity). Without daily sustenance, the Evangelist suffers **-1 cumulative penalty to all attack rolls and saves per day starved**. After 7 days without metal or oil, they enter forced dormancy (effectively unconscious) until fed.

The Evangelist gains no nutrition from biological food. They may consume it for taste or social reasons, but it provides nothing.

#### Valuable Sustenance

Consuming **at least 50 gp worth of metal or oil** in a single feeding (above and beyond the daily requirement) channels excess material into Phyrexian gifts. The Evangelist chooses one of the following effects:

- **Restore 1 spell slot**; 50 gp per spell slot per level -- 100 gp for two level one slots or one level two slot.
- **Gain temporary hp** equal to 1 Hit Die roll.

Each effect may be triggered **once per day, maximum**. The metal or oil is destroyed in the process.

#### Oil for Blood

The Evangelist does not bleed in the conventional sense. Instead, wounds leak black, oily fluid.

- **Immune to bleeding effects, blood-based curses, and vampiric drain.**
- **Heavy wounds leave oily tracks**: when below half hp, the Evangelist suffers **-2 to stealth and tracking-evasion** as they leave traces.
- **Mortals find the bleeding deeply unsettling.** Witnessing the Evangelist bleed openly may trigger reaction roll penalties at GM discretion.

#### Compleated Lungs

The Evangelist does not breathe air. They are **immune to suffocation, drowning, and gas-based attacks** (poison gas, stinking cloud, choking effects). They can function indefinitely underwater or in airless conditions. They may still speak, as their voice is produced through grafted mechanical resonators rather than lungs.

#### Mortal Revulsion (and Concealment)

The Evangelist's grafts and oily features are unsettling to mortals when exposed. **Concealment** is straightforward: a cloak, gloves, hood, mask, or full-body covering hides the Phyrexian nature in casual interaction. While concealed, the Evangelist passes for an unusual mortal (perhaps scarred, sickly, or just eccentric).

**When unconcealed in front of non-Phyrexians**:

- **-2 to reaction rolls** with mortals (humans, demi-humans, fey, most folk of Dolmenwood)
- **-2 to social skill checks** that rely on goodwill, charm, or trust (negotiation, persuasion, deception requiring rapport)
- **+2 to intimidation checks** when leveraging fear of the unknown

**Practical implications**: An Evangelist seeking to proselytize will often appear as a hooded stranger. Revealing their nature is a dramatic moment, used either to convert the curious or terrify the resistant. Some Evangelists develop reputations as "the cloaked preacher" or similar in the regions they work.
